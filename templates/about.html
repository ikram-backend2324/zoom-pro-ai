About Zoom

We believe great meetings shouldn't require an account, a download, or a tutorial.

Zoom is a platform built on one simple idea: connecting people should be frictionless. No sign-ups, no software installs, no complicated setup. Just create a room, share a code, and meet.

The Story

Built for humans,
not enterprises

Most video platforms are built for IT departments. Zoom was built for a student scrambling to set up a study group, a freelancer who just needs a quick call, a family trying to connect across continents. We stripped out everything unnecessary and kept only what matters: a room, a code, and the people you want to meet.
